\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An anchor of an item to which positions can be attached to. 

An item (\doxylink{classQCPAbstractItem}{QCPAbstract\+Item}) may have one or more anchors. Unlike \doxylink{classQCPItemPosition}{QCPItem\+Position}, an anchor doesn\textquotesingle{}t control anything on its item, but provides a way to tie other items via their positions to the anchor.

For example, a \doxylink{classQCPItemRect}{QCPItem\+Rect} is defined by its positions {\itshape top\+Left} and {\itshape bottom\+Right}. Additionally it has various anchors like {\itshape top}, {\itshape top\+Right} or {\itshape bottom\+Left} etc. So you can attach the {\itshape start} (which is a \doxylink{classQCPItemPosition}{QCPItem\+Position}) of a \doxylink{classQCPItemLine}{QCPItem\+Line} to one of the anchors by calling QCPItem\+Position\+::set\+Parent\+Anchor on {\itshape start}, passing the wanted anchor of the \doxylink{classQCPItemRect}{QCPItem\+Rect}. This way the start of the line will now always follow the respective anchor location on the rect item.

Note that \doxylink{classQCPItemPosition}{QCPItem\+Position} derives from \doxylink{classQCPItemAnchor}{QCPItem\+Anchor}, so every position can also serve as an anchor to other positions.

To learn how to provide anchors in your own item subclasses, see the subclassing section of the \doxylink{classQCPAbstractItem}{QCPAbstract\+Item} documentation.
